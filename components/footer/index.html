Footer

🔹 Needed for CIC Insurance Branding

The footer displays across the bottom of all CIC Insurance websites.

Figma

  • Guidance
  • Demo & Code

Usage

To be used across the bottom of all CIC Insurance websites.

Use the footer to provide links to content that are separate from the main navigation but that are relevant to the entire site and platform. For example: About us, Privacy statement, Terms of use, Accessibility, Legal content and contact information and links to social media.

Do:

  • only use option section links where they assist the user, otherwise they can be hidden
  • use a copyright statement that is specific to your agency.

Masterbrand corporate

Masterbrand corporate use the Default Footer (Grey 01) to maintain a strong and consistent connection to CIC Insurance.

Masterbrand non-corporate, co-brand and independent

Masterbrand non-corporate, co-brand and independent is preferred to use the Default, however can also use the Brand palette variant to utilise a different brand colour.

Endorsed and stand-alone

Endorsed and stand-alone can utilise the Brand palette variant to apply a custom colour as desired.

Component anatomy

Mega footer (optional)
A section for additional internal and external links to help users continue their journey.

Acknowledgement of Country
The Acknowledgement of Country pays respect to the First Peoples of CIC.

Footer links (optional)
Content that sits outside the main navigation but that is relevant to the entire site and platform.

Copyright
A required statement that applies to all pages of the site.

Social icons (optional)
Links to the organisation's official social media accounts.

Attribution text (optional)
A short note to acknowledge the team, organisation or system behind the product.

Accessibility

All components are responsive and designed to comply with WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards. Full compliance depends on using and configuring the components correctly.
