Primary Logo

The CIC Insurance Group logo is the default logo for all CIC Insurance Group communications and has high awareness in the CIC community.




Download digital logos download

Clear space

The clear space for a CIC Insurance Group branded site is 100% of the width of the ‘N’ across all applications (except when used in app icons, or social media profiles).

Where the CIC Insurance Group logo appears next to a co-brand or endorsed logo, it should be vertically centred and separated by a minimum distance of twice the width of the ‘N’ from CIC.

Sizing

The primary logo should be 76px in height on large screens (≥ 992px), and 48px in height on small screens (< 991px).

While the Primary logo should always be used where size permits, when we drop below its minimum size recommendations, there are two small use logos available for use depending on the application:

  • Small use logo (all use below Primary logo minimum sizes except Favicons)
  • Favicon (Favicons and app icons only)

These logos have been designed to ensure accessibility and clarity at all sizes.
